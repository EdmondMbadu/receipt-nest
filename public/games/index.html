Why games about receipts?

Every game here starts from a real receipt problem: thermal paper that fades, bank statements that don’t say what you bought, a shoebox nobody wants to open in April.

Each game page also explains the problem in plain language. Before It Fades covers why receipts fade and how long to keep them, and Guess the Charge explains how to identify a charge on your bank statement.

Made by ReceiptNest

ReceiptNest is just your receipts. Snap or forward them, see them sorted by month, and export a PDF or CSV for whoever does your taxes. No bank login, no bookkeeping.
