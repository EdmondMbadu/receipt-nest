Real app walkthrough · Captions included

Prefer to read? Everything you need is below. Download the video.

01 / ADD A RECEIPT

Snap it. Or upload it.

On your dashboard, choose Add Receipt. On a phone, use the + button. Scan a paper receipt, or choose Upload Existing File for a photo or PDF.

Check the preview, then choose Process Receipt.

02 / AUTOMATICALLY ORGANIZED

The details, handled.

ReceiptNest reads the merchant, amount and date, assigns a category, and saves the receipt. For most receipts, that’s it.

Edit details or add a note anytime. If a receipt is marked Needs Review, check it and choose Confirm & Save.

03 / FIND & DOWNLOAD

Ready when you need it.

Search by merchant, amount or date. Open a receipt, then choose Download below Original Receipt to save the original file.

For a whole month, use its Download menu: CSV for merchant, date, amount and a total; PDF for receipt documents in one file. PDF export is a Pro feature.

One more shortcut: forward email receipts.

Copy your personal receipt address from the dashboard and forward emailed receipts there. ReceiptNest processes those automatically too.
